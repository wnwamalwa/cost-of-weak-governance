What does weak governance cost Kenya?

Corruption and weak accountability widen the budget deficit, raise power and transport costs, hold back firms, and tax the earnings of informal workers. This dashboard puts the World Bank’s published estimates in shillings, jobs and debt.

How to read this dashboard. The headline converts the World Bank’s estimate (governance constraints widen the deficit by about 2% of GDP a year) into shillings at the latest nominal GDP; it is an order of magnitude, not an audited loss. The channel figures are not added together, to avoid double counting. The ticker spreads the annual estimate evenly over the year. Debt, GDP and population come live from the Kenya National Debt Counter when it can be reached.
